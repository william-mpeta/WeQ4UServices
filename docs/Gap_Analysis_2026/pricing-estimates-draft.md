# Internal draft: service-fee estimates

**Status:** Working estimates for founder discussion only. Not approved customer prices. Prepared 23 October 2026 from the available workflow notes and a small public competitor sample. Recalculate after recording actual staff time, travel, failed visits, payment costs and delivery costs.

## Quote structure

Show these items separately in every quote:

1. **WeQ4UServices handling fee** for the specific transaction.
2. **Authority / statutory charges** at the amount confirmed for that vehicle, applicant and authority. Do not mark these up or guess them.
3. **Third-party charges** such as medical certificates, fingerprint processing, roadworthy inspection, repairs or retests, each identified separately.
4. **Courier charge**, quoted by route and number of collections/deliveries. Charge a shared courier leg once when multiple services travel together.
5. **Complexity or extra-visit charge**, only when the reason and amount have been explained and accepted before the extra work.

Suggested internal floor calculation:

`handling fee = (staff time cost + direct transaction cost + allocated overhead) / (1 - target gross margin)`

Use a measured loaded hourly staff cost, including employer costs; add actual travel/parking/printing/bank charges; allocate monthly overhead across completed paid jobs. The founder should choose the target margin after reviewing real monthly volumes and the business's tax/VAT position. Revisit the fee after the first 20-30 completed jobs.

If the case cannot be priced from a known checklist and route, issue a quote after review instead of selecting a number from this table.

## Important scope and controls

- These are internal estimates. Keep them out of the public site and chatbot until the founder approves a fee schedule.
- Confirm the business is allowed to perform each booking / facilitation step through the proposed channel. Do not promise a slot, faster government processing, a pass, or an issue date.
- The workflow notes mention a temporary PrDP fee of R70. It has not been verified as the current authority fee; do not repeat or include it in a quote until confirmed.
- The service list includes more transaction types than the current eight website categories. Treat registration, deregistration, address changes, title-holder changes, NCO, company registration and traffic-register-number work as distinct quote lines where appropriate.
- Do not ask customers to send identity documents by ordinary email or chat. Confirm the minimum necessary documents and approved secure route first.
- Decide how to handle quotes for bank-financed transfers, dealership transfers, late renewals, arrears, missing documents, rejected applications, cancellations, repeat visits, refunds, VAT and payment collection.

## Comparator sources reviewed

- [DRP Plates: Products & Pricing](https://www.drplates.co.za/products-pricing/) lists Gauteng provider service fees of R170 for a licence renewal, R200 for licence and registration, R200 for NCO, R170 for change of details, R200 for title change and R200 for deregistration; courier is listed at R200. Authority charges are extra.
- [Cathy's: Change of Ownership](https://www.cathys.co.za/change-of-ownerships) lists its Johannesburg Q4You handling fee at R300, with licensing department fees and annual licence charges shown separately.
- [Qline: Change of Ownership](https://qline.co.za/service/change-of-ownership-of-vehicle-service-fee/) lists a R395 service fee and says department fees and delivery are separate.
- [News24: Natis booking assistance](https://www.news24.com/life/motoring/opinions/cant-get-a-licence-slot-heres-how-you-can-get-help-for-the-troublesome-natis-system-20210511-3) reported one provider charging R200 for learner, driver and PrDP bookings in 2021. Treat this as an old reference point only.

These are a few advertised prices, not a market average. They support initial discussion bands for motor-vehicle administration; they do not validate the licence, PrDP, duplicate-document or roadworthy bands. Current government charges and the permitted service workflow must be checked for each case.
