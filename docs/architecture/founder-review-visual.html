View 1 · Service journey

Enquiry to completion

Keep the customer informed at each stage. Each step creates a visible status or an action for the customer or team.

Choose one service; give contact details.

02 · CUSTOMER + TEAMDocuments

Upload what is available; team checks gaps.

03 · STAFFVerify & quote

View 2 · Database starting point

Design around the request and its history

The logical ERD and PostgreSQL table plan exist in the architecture document. These are the first data groups to validate before we write migrations.

People & request

customers → service_requests → request_services
Contact snapshots, source, consent, public reference, selected service and answers.

Documents & work

request_documents → document_reviews
workflow_instances → workflow_tasks → request_events
Store file metadata in PostgreSQL; keep file bytes in private object storage.

Commercial & governance

quotes → quote_lines → quote_decisions
payment_orders / transactions, audit_events, notifications and retention records.

CUSTOMER ──< REQUEST ──< REQUEST_SERVICE ──< DOCUMENT ├──< WORKFLOW_INSTANCE ──< TASK ──< TASK_EVENT ├──< REQUEST_EVENT └──< QUOTE ──< QUOTE_LINE ──< PAYMENT_ORDER Staff, departments, service/checklist/workflow versions, notifications and audit records connect to the relevant case and action.

View 3 · Founder decisions

Agree the operating rules before database build

Suggested meeting outcome: approve the first service workflow and database slice; record unresolved commercial and policy questions as decisions, not assumptions.

1What is the authoritative status flow?

Agree the names and who can move a request through each step.

2Which service should be the pilot?

Choose one service with known documents, exceptions and completion evidence.

3Who does each task?

Confirm roles for customer support, back office, supervisor, founder and system administrator.

4What does a quote contain?

Separate WeQ4U fees, authority charges, third-party costs and optional courier costs.

5When is payment due?

Confirm service-specific payment milestones. Pricing can be finalized later.

6What records must be kept?

Agree who can see documents, what is audited and who approves retention rules.

Design proposal for founder review. Not a government-system integration or a claim of production readiness.Next: validate one real request end to end, then approve core schema and migration.
