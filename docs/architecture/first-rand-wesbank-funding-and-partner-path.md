# FirstRand / WesBank partnership and funding path

**Status:** Internal preparation note, 5 November 2026. No approval, referral arrangement or partnership is assumed.

## Separate the two conversations

WeQ4UServices has two different proposals to validate:

1. **Business capability funding:** capital to build and operate the service platform. The conversation belongs with an appropriate FNB Business relationship manager or FirstRand enterprise-development route, subject to their eligibility and assessment.
2. **Customer-service pilot:** permission to support a defined post-purchase workflow for consenting WesBank or dealer customers. This requires an operational sponsor, documented scope, data-sharing terms, customer authorization and a controlled process. A funding conversation does not create this authorization.

The public site now uses a neutral `/partners` page to explain the pilot concept, the current prototype boundary, and measures to agree. It does not use partner logos or claim FirstRand, WesBank, BMW, Mercedes-Benz or a dealer as a customer or partner.

## What official information suggests

- FNB Business publicly describes patient growth capital for early-stage and profitable businesses, supplier-development contributions for FirstRand suppliers, and Vumela enterprise-development finance. These are routes to ask about, not evidence that WeQ4UServices qualifies or will receive funding. Start with the company's relationship manager and request the current criteria, application route, evidence requirements, and whether a technology-enabled service business fits. [FNB Business - Funding and supplier development](https://online.firstrand.co.za/business-banking/enterprise-supplier-development/funding.html)
- WesBank's customer guidance explains that titleholder and NaTIS handling can depend on whether vehicle finance is still active or has been settled. Its third-party request form requires a customer authorization and identifies customer and third-party identity documents for that particular request process. Do not turn this into a blanket document checklist: confirm the current provider requirements and collect sensitive documents only after scope, authority, secure transfer and purpose are agreed. [WesBank - Transferring ownership to your name](https://www.wesbank.co.za/help-centre/new-to-wesbank/transferring-ownership-to-your-name) · [WesBank - Third Party Request form](https://www.wesbank.co.za/SiteBranding/documents/coza/WesBank_Third_Party_Request.pdf)
- WesBank describes affiliated dealership workflows for finance applications and vehicle handover. That does not establish an API, permission for WeQ4UServices to act on behalf of customers, or an endorsed post-sale service. [WesBank - Buying from a dealership](https://www.wesbank.co.za/help-centre/becoming-a-customer/buying-from-a-dealership)

## Evidence package before a funding meeting

Prepare a concise, evidence-led data room:

- Current prototype walkthrough with a clearly labelled demo dataset; no personal customer documents.
- Founder-approved operating scope and a single pilot workflow, including exceptions and named accountability.
- Development roadmap separating what exists (enquiry intake, private reference tracking, staff request queue) from what requires investment (PostgreSQL, production identity/RBAC, partner referral, consent records, audit trail, secure production document storage, monitoring and backups).
- A 12-month budget with assumptions, runway, build and operational cost, support capacity, security/privacy work, and proposed use of funds.
- A unit-economics worksheet with separate WeQ4U fees and authority, lender, dealer, courier or other pass-through charges. Use real founder figures; do not infer revenue from deck examples.
- Pilot measurement plan: opt-in, first-review document completeness, elapsed time by stage, follow-up contacts, rework, exceptions, completion, customer feedback, cost to serve, and privacy/security incidents.
- Evidence and method for each market claim in the confidential WeBuyCars deck. Treat the stated review themes, time saved, and operational impact as hypotheses until their source, sample, method and partner context are independently reviewed.

## Suggested sequence

1. Validate the actual customer and dealer workflow with the founder and one willing retail-operations contact.
2. Ask the existing FNB Business relationship manager which current business-funding route and eligibility criteria fit the company; do not assume direct FirstRand Group grant funding.
3. Separately ask a WesBank or dealer operations contact whether a discovery session is appropriate, what customer authorization is required, and who owns the workflow and data.
4. Agree a bounded pilot and baseline before quoting benefits, service levels, funding impact or scaling plans.
5. Build production controls and partner boundaries before handling real partner referrals or lender-sensitive documents.

## Product safeguards

- WeQ4UServices is a document-support and service-coordination concept, not a lender, credit broker, credit assessor or substitute for a lender, dealer or licensing authority.
- Do not collect finance account numbers, full VINs, ID copies or bank statements in the introductory enquiry. Ask for sensitive records only through a purpose-bound, authorized and secured process.
- A customer's consent to contact WeQ4UServices is not automatically consent to disclose the customer's information to a dealer or lender. Capture separate, named, purpose-specific authorization before any partner disclosure.
- Do not promise credit access, vehicle approval, zero visits, fixed turnaround or guaranteed savings.
