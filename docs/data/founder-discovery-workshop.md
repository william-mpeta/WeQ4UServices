# Founder Discovery Workshop

Use this before finalizing the Supabase schema. The goal is to capture how the service is actually run today, not to force the manual process into a guessed software workflow.

## Suggested session

Plan a 60-90 minute walkthrough with the founder. Pick one recent, ordinary request and trace it from first contact to completed delivery. Then review edge cases for the remaining services.

## Capture for each service

- How the customer contacts the business
- First questions asked and information needed to decide whether to help
- Confirmed document checklist and when each document is requested
- Gauteng coverage and collection/delivery availability rule
- How office/statutory fees, service fee and courier charge are determined
- Who performs the next step and how completion is verified
- Customer update points and preferred channel
- Typical delays, rejection reasons and exceptions
- Cancellation, refund, complaint and document-deletion handling

## Confirm business decisions

- Approved service descriptions and public wording
- Operating hours and callback expectations
- Who may draft, approve and send a quote
- Quote expiry, acceptance and payment methods
- Status names and who is allowed to change each status
- Document access, retention period, deletion request process and audit expectations
- Whether customers may opt into renewal reminders and the exact consent wording

## Government booking integration discovery

Identify the exact authority, office or authorized intermediary for each booking. Ask whether there is an official integration program, written terms, API documentation, sandbox, authentication method, permitted data fields, rate limits, support contact and production approval process. Until these are confirmed, keep staff-operated processing as the source of truth and do not automate portals or reuse personal credentials.

## Outputs

- Approved as-is workflow with exception paths
- Approved service catalogue and service-area rule
- Service-by-service checklist with owner and approval date
- Quote and payment decision record
- Status transition table and staff permissions
- Data retention and access policy requiring appropriate privacy/legal review
- List of external systems and confirmed integration evidence
